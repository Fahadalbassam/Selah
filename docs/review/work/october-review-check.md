## From the research folder

**D8 (department limits, and how to rebuild the agenda)**

- `inputs/research/selah-multi-department.html`, "Cross-department recommendations": "A department's people see their action and the recommendation's title; the recommendation's progress counts all its actions." This fits Gate A: a title of a company-wide recommendation, a count, no other department's action titles.
- Same file, "Sessions and consultants": "Selah should let a meeting carry a department (so the agenda shows only that department's open actions) and let a steering meeting pull open items from every active project of the client." This is already built: `apps/web/drizzle/0021_departments_across.sql` ("A meeting may be a department's, and its agenda then covers that department's work") and `docs/rules.md:346`. So there is a third option for the agenda, cheaper than a live list per reader: the agenda follows the meeting. A department's meeting lists that department; a whole-project meeting lists everything and is opened only by people who may see everything.
- Same file, "The people" and `inputs/research/platforms/03-psa-lifecycle.md:256`: the sponsor "chairs steering"; the steering committee does "monthly trade-offs"; "senior sponsors oversee direction, scope, budget, timeline ... agenda set in advance". A sponsor or committee member who is limited to one department cannot do that job under the Gate A rule. This is the cost of the strict option and belongs in the side-by-side Fahad asked for.
- Same file, "How comparable products model it": "client users see what their project membership allows, and only GRC tools narrow by unit". Most products do not limit by department at all. Selah's promise is the stricter one; no product studied shows a shared agenda cut per reader.
- `inputs/research/platforms/04-governance-strategy.md:263`: "Separate 'Spaces' for board, family council, shareholders, departments with their own membership and visibility ... visibility is per space, not per company." And line 236: "on a department page, show only that unit's actions ... on a project page, show that project's actions regardless of department." Both support "scope by the place" (the meeting, the page) over "scope by the reader".
- `inputs/research/platforms/01-portals.md:192` (Accelo): "visibility choices per item ('all / only where I'm observer or approver / none') ... show an action owner only actions they own." Supports closing N1 and N5.
