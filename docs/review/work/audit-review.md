### Answers to the questions asked in the brief

- **Action owner without the Projects row.** The project page stays reachable through a link on an action. Lost as written: requests addressed to the owner, meetings to answer, any project where the owner has no action (finding 4). Shared files stay in the Files card. The recommendation stays a link on the action.
- **Managers without Team and Departments.** Nowhere in the target shows a manager who heads a department (finding 8).
- **Activity for clients.** Nothing single lives only there; the project-wide record of the firm's steps does (finding 20).
- **Oversight.** "Client last active" feeds nothing else on screen. The 8-week history is only on Oversight and is made of the health word (finding 13).
- **Today into Home.** All six sections are named in 4.2. "My own actions" inside the due section is dropped (finding 26). The admin's scope is D6 (finding 22).
- **Needs attention as chips.** No: overdue and disputed survive; stale, slipped, churn and no active owner do not (finding 7).
- **Closing as a menu item.** The client admin and the sponsor can still ask, and a lead can still start. The pending request has no place (finding 16).
