## Checked and found sound

- **Migrations.** All five apply in order on an empty database, and `drizzle-kit check` passes; the journal and snapshots line up. Each replaced definition keeps everything the previous one did:
  - `app_can_see_meeting` (baseline 4168): keeps the project and label checks and adds the department check.
  - `scoped_read ON activity_events` (baseline 1630): keeps project and label and adds the role check. `scoped_insert` is unchanged.
  - `app_meeting_agenda(…, dept)` (0021:96): only the date and status formatting changed; the shared-recommendation join, the drafts excluded (`app_action_open`) and the department filter are kept. Neither form is executable by `selah_app` (catalog).
  - `client_kpis` writer insert, update and delete, `client_kpi_links` writer insert and delete, `client_kpi_values` report and firm check, `indicator_imports` report (0063, 0067): every condition is kept. `app_org_kind() = 'firm'` is implied by `app_firm_works_with`, and the client-role branch is unchanged.
  - `app_kpi_data_admin` and `app_propose_kpi_data_mode` (0067): the only change is `app_firm_works_with` to `app_firm_changes_kpis`.
  - Grants are kept by `CREATE OR REPLACE`.
  - Every new definer function sets `search_path`, revokes PUBLIC, grants only `selah_app`, and checks the caller itself (`app_can_see_project`, label, department, `app_can_see_action`, `app_can_see_meeting`). Called with another client's ids, they answer nothing (test lines 264-270, 291-294, 384-385, 432-438; my probe of the other client's admin).
- **The department meeting has no other road.** Every read of `meetings`, `meeting_attendees`, `meeting_decisions`, `meeting_summaries`, `meeting_summary_sources`, `follow_ups` and `meeting_notes` goes through the app role under RLS. The only views over these tables (`v_schedule`, `v_deviations`, `v_project_metrics`) are `security_invoker=true`. The definer functions the app can execute that read meeting tables are `app_attends_meeting`, `app_can_run_meeting`, `app_can_see_meeting`, `app_meeting_agenda_for`, `app_meeting_open_to_me` and `app_send_meeting_invites` (guarded by `app_can_run_meeting`), plus triggers. The calendar file uses `loadMeeting`. The invite mail job sends only to attendees. Assistant passages of meeting notes are firm-only by policy `assistant_on` (0061). The Files card's meeting source is read under RLS.
- **The meeting with no department is unchanged** for everyone on the project (test lines 316-317, 336-337).
- **Booking.** The service and `department_book` both refuse a manager booking for a department she is not in. The firm, the client admin and the department's own people still book.
- **Invited guest of a department meeting.** She reads the meeting and its attendees, decisions, summary and follow-ups (`app_attends_meeting`), and her RSVP passes the `respond` policy. The agenda is withheld from her, by design.
- **Revoking `meetings.agenda_md`.** Every `agenda_md`, `select()`, `from(meetings)`, `FROM meetings` and `meetings.*` in `src/`, `scripts/` and `e2e/` was checked. `loadMeeting` uses explicit columns, `listMeetings` selects columns, `updateMeeting` returns only `icsSequence`, and `today.ts` goes through `app_meeting_agenda_for()`. The mail job and `app_purge_minimal_records` run as the owner. The assistant reads `title` and `starts_at`. The walkthrough seed books, updates and invites through the services as the lead. The full DB suite passes.
- **Activity record.** No function or view that serves client managers reads `activity_events`: `app_status_pack` is called for firm drafts and by the job, and `app_client_last_active` is firm-only. Nothing inserts into `activity_events` with RETURNING. The action page's refiling line still reaches everyone who may open the action (test lines 375-386).
- **Agenda reader rule.** The firm, the client admin, a manager with no department limit and a department's own people read it; action owners and limited managers get the one line, and the text never reaches them (test lines 396-439).
- **Observer.** App and database agree for the firm admin, the lead, the deputy, a staffed consultant, an observer, and a team member staffed nowhere. Client roles are unchanged. The meeting page draws no run, follow-up or RSVP control for an observer, and the assessments tab draws no "Start" (the DB refuses all of these; test lines 331-342).
- **Schedule for action owners** (`/api/schedule`): only their own actions and follow-ups. Meetings are unchanged.
- **i18n.** Every new key exists in both `ar.json` and `en.json` under the same path. The Arabic plural forms (`one`, `two`, `few`, `many`, `other`) of `actionsLeftOut`, `statusPack.leftOut` and `indicators.report.recsLeftOut` are right; `zero` is not needed because each is shown only when n > 0.
- **Agenda wording.** `app_action_status_label()` matches `messages/*.json` `status.*` exactly (test 455-463). Old agendas are mapped when drawn, never rewritten (`lib/agenda.ts`, `tests/agenda.test.ts`).
- **Assessments tab.** `total` (and so the "suggested" line) is computed only for the firm (`assessments.ts:794`).
- **Departments page.** `canOpen` matches `departmentOverview()` for the firm, the client admin and managers.
- **Draft rule (D-later).** No new place shows a draft to a client, and nothing here hard-codes drafts as visible in a way the later RLS change would have to undo, except the assertion in #14.
