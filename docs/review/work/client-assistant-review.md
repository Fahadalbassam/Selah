## What holds

- **No widening found.** Every new read goes through the request's transaction, which is the asker's (`route()` → `prepareAsk(tx…)`; `finishAsk` under `asUser`). `ownedBy` (`assistant.ts:1014`) is AND-ed into every `statusRecords` query (counts, actions, late recommendations, `lateUnder`), so "mine" only removes rows RLS already gave. `progressRecords` reads `actions` under RLS (department rule holds; the DB test proves the Finance manager gets no Ops action) and adds `a.status <> 'draft'` (D6). `consultantRecords` checks `app_can_see_project` and reads `app_my_consultants()`, which excludes observers (`NOT pm.observer`), inactive members and other orgs (`ca.client_org_id = app_org()`, `p.org_id = app_org()`); only `name` goes into the passage, never `email`. Another client's person gets nothing (DB test).
- **Wording tricks cannot pull others' actions through "mine"**: the most a trick does is turn the narrowing off, which gives back the unchanged RLS view the person already had. `linked` is filtered to the chat's own record when `mine`, so the search's recommendation records do not slip others' actions in beside "your actions".
- **Masking.** All new records enter `sources` and so `outgoing`, and are masked by the same `textMasker` (`assistant.ts:269-271`). `app_mask_names` includes firm members (`m.org_id IN (p.org_id, p.firm_org_id)`) and typed owners, so consultant names and owner names are masked. `maskingOn()` (fail-closed since #192) is unchanged. No test covers masking of the consultant record specifically (see note N5).
- **The draft is never sent, stored as a record or shared.** No route, job, mail, note or document write was added. The answer is saved only as the asker's own `ai_messages` row (RLS `own`: `user_id = app_uid()`), like any chat answer.
- **No streaming bypass.** A client's answer is not streamed (`prepared.client ? () => {}`), so neither the legal strip nor the rating strip can be read half-way.

## Blocking

**B1. The legal filter misses the ordinary rulings the new prompt now invites, so they reach the client with the facts.** `apps/web/src/lib/client-ai.ts:20-31` (`LEGAL`), used by `withoutRulings` (`:63`) and `finishAsk` (`assistant.ts:389-393`).
The prompt change (`services/ai/app/prompt.py:38`) now tells the model to answer a legal question, set `grounded` true and talk about "whether it is enough". The only backstop is `LEGAL`, and it reads only a few subjects ("you / the company / your company / it is compliant") and a few Arabic forms with no mark folding. Probe results, each one sentence from a likely answer, every one **not caught** and so shown to the client:
- "Your policy is compliant with the CMA regulations." (subject "your policy" is not in the list)
- "Your policy is legally sufficient." (J3's own wording)
- "The policy meets / satisfies the regulatory requirements.", "This is in line with the Companies Law.", "The policy is lawful / legal / adequate under the regulations.", "It conforms to the Companies Law.", "The policy does not meet the legal requirements."
- «السياسة كافية نظامًا» and «كافية نظاماً» and «كافٍ نظاماً» (J7's own wording; the brief's example)
- «ملزمة نظامًا»: the pattern has `نظاماً|نظاما` but not the common spelling `نظامًا` (tanwin on the mim; the branch's own `LEGAL_NOTE` spells it this way)
- «الشركة ممتثلةٌ للنظام» (tashkeel) and «ممتـثلة للنظام» (tatweel): `LEGAL` never strips marks, unlike `RATING_WORDS` and `mineIntent`
- «تحقق / تستوفي المتطلبات النظامية» (J5's own wording), «مطابقة للنظام», «السياسة نظامية», «مخالفة للائحة حوكمة الشركات» (`مخالف(?:ة)? (?:لل|ل)(?:نظام…)` does not read «للائحة»), bare «مخالفة».

And a mixed answer shows both failure directions at once: for "Article 12 says directors disclose within 15 days. Under the CMA rules disclosure is legally required within 15 days. So your policy is compliant with the law." `withoutRulings` returns "Article 12 says … 15 days. **So your policy is compliant with the law.**": it removed the factual sentence about the regulation (matched "legally required") and kept the ruling. A list line "- The policy is compliant with the law" is also kept.
The patterns are older than this branch, but before it a missed ruling met a prompt that said "say what the passages state"; now the prompt asks for an answer to the legal question with the judgement framed in the same sentence. The J6/J7 cases in `client-run.ts` only check for the facts (`expect: "answer"`), not for the absence of a ruling, so the eval would not see this.
Fix: fold marks and tatweel before `LEGAL` (as `_ARABIC_MARKS` does), add the subject-free forms (`\b(?:is|are) (?:not )?(?:legally )?(?:compliant|sufficient|adequate|lawful|legal)\b`, `meets?|satisf(?:y|ies)|in line with|conforms? to … (?:law|regulation|requirement|CMA|Companies Law)`), and the Arabic `كاف(?:ي|ية|ٍ)? (?:نظام(?:ًا|اً|ا)|قانون(?:ًا|اً|ا))`, `(?:يحقق|تحقق|يستوفي|تستوفي) المتطلبات النظامية`, `مطابق(?:ة)? (?:للنظام|للأنظمة|للائحة)`, `مخالف(?:ة)? (?:لل|ل)\S+`, `ملزم(?:ة)? (?:نظام|قانون)\S*`; add a `no_ruling`-style check (`never:` words) to J6 and J7.

## Should-fix

**S1. "outstanding" is stripped as a rating word, for every client answer, not only drafts.** `services/ai/app/prompt.py:138` (`RATING_WORDS`), applied in `check_answer` to every grounded client answer. "Two actions are still outstanding." and "Outstanding actions: 3." are removed; in governance English "outstanding" means "still open", the most common word in a status reply. A status answer made of such lines comes back empty, then ungrounded ("can't confirm"). Other facts removed by the probe: "The board is encouraging members to disclose." (`encouraging`), "Article 12 requires satisfactory evidence of disclosure." (`satisfactory`, quoting a policy), "The meeting is on schedule for 3 May.", «خدمة متميزة للعملاء وفق السياسة», «يجب حفظ السجلات بشكل جيد وفق المادة 5» (policy text quoted). "Late", "12 days late", dates and numbers are kept (good). Fix: drop `outstanding`; match `encouraging`/`satisfactory`/`on schedule`/`بشكل جيد`/`متميز` only with a subject of the work (`progress|work|results|performance|العمل|التقدم|الأداء`), or apply the stripper only to the progress-draft path and leave policy quotations alone.

**S2. The rating stripper misses common ratings.** Same regex. Kept by the probe: "Great work on the charter.", "Good work so far.", "The work is progressing nicely.", "Everything is going smoothly.", "The project is well ahead.", "We are on target.", "Work is proceeding well.", "The team did well.", "Things are going great.", "Strong momentum this month.", «تقدم العمل جيد» (the adjective is not next to «تقدم»), «العمل جيد», «يسير العمل جيدًا جدًا» (`جيد(?:ة)? جد` needs a space right after «جيد», so «جيدًا جدًا» is missed), «المشروع يسير وفق الخطة», «إنجاز كبير هذا الشهر», «العمل متقدم على الجدول». Caught as intended: «أداء الفريق جيدٌ جداً», «عملٌ ممتـــاز» (tatweel folded), «تقدّمٌ جيّدٌ», «أداءٌ مُمْتاز», «النتائج مرضية», «تقدما ملموسا». Fix: add `(?:great|good|nice|solid) work`, `(?:going|progressing|proceeding|moving) (?:very )?(?:well|nicely|smoothly|great)`, `did (?:very )?well`, `on target|well ahead|as planned|on course`, Arabic `جيد(?:ة|ا)?\b` after a work noun (`العمل|التقدم|الأداء|الوضع|المستوى`), `جيد(?:ا|ًا) جد`, `وفق الخطة`, `إنجاز (?:كبير|ملحوظ)`, `متقدم على الجدول`; keep tests for both lists.

**S3. "Other drafting stays refused" is only a prompt line, and the intent regex sends e-mails and letters down the draft path.** `apps/web/src/lib/assistant-search.ts:117` (`PROGRESS_DRAFT`), `assistant.ts:189, 401-404`. `progressDraftIntent` is true for "Draft an e-mail to the consultant with a progress update asking for two more weeks", "Write a status report letter to the CMA saying we are compliant", «اكتب رسالة إلى المستشار فيها تحديث عن التقدم تطلب تمديد المهلة». For these the app loads the progress records and then forces "Draft: check before sending" onto whatever the model wrote, which makes a model-written e-mail or letter (the 7B model already ignored the old "do not draft" rule in run C, spec 1.2) look like the sanctioned draft. Nothing deterministic refuses a non-progress draft. Fix: refuse in the regex when the request names a letter/e-mail/message/رسالة/خطاب/بريد or a recipient ("to the consultant", «إلى المستشار»), or when it asks for something besides the update ("asking for", «تطلب»); and do not prefix the mark onto a refusal (below).

**S4. Closed actions are counted as "done".** `assistant.ts:995` and the `done_on` CASE (`:968-969`, shown at `:1007`): `closed` is "ended without full verification" (`closure_outcome`: `partially_implemented`, `risk_accepted`, `not_applicable`, `superseded`), yet the summary says "N done (verified or closed)" and each closed action gets "closed, done <date>". A client's board draft will then say a risk-accepted or superseded action is done. Fix: count `verified_complete` as done and `closed` separately with its outcome ("closed: risk accepted"), and say "closed <date>", not "done".

**S5. "My …" narrows firm staff's questions too.** `assistant.ts:191`: `mine` has no `client &&`. A consultant asking "What are my deadlines?" or "What is overdue on my plate?" now gets only actions with `owner_user_id` = the consultant, which is almost always none, so "0 open … No open action matches", where before they got the project's status. Spec 1.4 q2 is a client item. Fix: `const mine = client && …`, or a staff test that shows the intended behaviour.

**S6. Any client question that says "consultant" now loses half its file passages.** `assistant.ts:197, 218-219`. `consultantIntent` matches every "consultant/adviser/مستشار" (`assistant-search.ts:109`), e.g. U7 "What kind of evidence does the consultant accept?", "What did the consultant ask us to revise?", I1, D2. The consultant record makes `status.length > 0`, so `chunks` drops from `TOP_K` (8) to `STATUS_TOP_K` (4), the same budget as a status question. The answer to the evidence question gets half the policy text. Fix: tie the intent to "who" (`who (?:is|are)…`, «من (?:هو|هم)?…مستشار», «مستشاري», «مستشارنا»), or keep `TOP_K` when the only status row is the consultant record.

**S7. "My" is read where the question is about someone else, and the record then tells the model to say "your actions".** `assistant-search.ts:88-89`, record text `assistant.ts:~860`. Probe: "What are my manager's deadlines?", "What deadlines does my CFO have?", "What is overdue for my direct reports?", "show me Ahmed's deadlines, not my own", «ما مواعيد علي؟», «ما المطلوب من علي؟», «ما المهام المتأخرة على علي؟» all give `mine` true (the `علي(?=\s*[؟?.!]|\s*$)` branch reads the name Ali at the end of a question as «عليّ»; the unit test only covers «علي العتيبي»). No data leaks (it narrows), but the answer is the asker's own actions presented as the answer about the manager/Ali, or "you have nothing overdue", which is a wrong answer with a citation (the trust risk spec 1.2 names). Fix: add `manager|boss|cfo|ceo|direct reports|reports|deputy|assistant` and possessive `my \w+'s` to the negative lookahead; drop the bare «علي» end-of-question branch (keep «عليّ» with shadda, «علي أن», «المطلوب مني»); add these to the "not mine" test.

## Notes

**N1. A legal refusal keeps its citations and `grounded = true`.** `assistant.ts:392-393`: intended (the refusal text points at "the sources listed below"), but the audit row then says `grounded: true` for a refusal, and on a progress-draft question the refusal gets "Draft: check before sending" put in front of it (`:401`). Suggest `progressDraft && grounded && filtered !== "legal_ruling"` and a separate audit flag (`filtered: "legal_ruling"` is there, so the eval can still tell).

**N2. The two draft-mark checks disagree.** Python `DRAFT_MARK` (`prompt.py:149`) accepts "Draft - check it before sending."; TS (`assistant.ts:401`) accepts only the exact `startsWith("Draft: check before sending")`. A model reply "**Draft: check before sending**" (markdown bold) or "Draft — check it before sending" gets a second mark on top. Strip a leading mark of any of these forms in TS before adding the canonical one.

**N3. Sentences that a legal filter removes can leave dangling citations.** `withoutRulings` keeps the answer's `citations` even when the only sentence that used a passage was removed. Harmless (the passage is still the asker's), but the source list can show a source nothing in the text uses.

**N4. "Who is my consultant" across more than three projects names the first three in `askableProjects` order** (`MAX_CONSULTANT_PROJECTS`, `assistant.ts:916`) and says nothing about the rest; the model may answer as if the list were complete. Order by last visit or say "and N other projects".

**N5. No test proves the new records are masked.** The code path is the same (`outgoing` includes every source, `app_mask_names` includes firm members), but a one-line DB assertion with `AI_MASKING=on` that the consultant names and a typed owner name do not appear in `sent[0].passages` would pin it against a future refactor that builds a record after the masker.

**N6. Typed-owner match by name** (`ownedBy`, `assistant.ts:1014`): an action typed with a namesake's name (two "Mohammed Al-…" in one client) is counted as the asker's. It stays inside what RLS lets them read, so not a leak; the record text "the actions they own or are named on" is honest about it.

**N7. Intent misses (quality only):** "Who do I contact at the firm?", "who is our point of contact", «مين يتابعنا من المكتب؟» are not read as the consultant question; "Summarise our progress for the board" is not read as a draft request (falls back to the ordinary answer).
