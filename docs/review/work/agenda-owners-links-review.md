**1b, in the database**
- The restrictive `owner` policy exists on `actions` and on all 14 tables with an `action_id` the app can read, plus `activity_events`, `content_translations` and `notifications` (SELECT). Catalog: every foreign key into `actions` and its child tables is covered. `follow_up_log` and `audit_log` are not readable by the app. `ai_chunks` is unreadable for owners through `assistant_on` and the `client_ai_roles` CHECK. `ai_drafts` is firm-only.
- All 180 SECURITY DEFINER functions the app can call are listed. Those that read actions and are reachable by an owner either refuse or filter: `app_can_see_action` (and through it the ladder, people, extensions and refilings), `app_second_review_state`, `app_actions_left_out`, `app_findings_answered_elsewhere`, `app_user_sees_action` (owner branch, older), `app_report_pack_for` (refuses owners), `app_team_*` (firm admin), `app_closure_blockers` (firm), `app_client_group_rollup` (group viewers), `app_person_holdings` (people managers). Exceptions are in finding 5.
- Counts move only with her own work. `v_schedule`, `v_project_metrics` and `v_deviations` are `security_invoker`. `app_project_progress`, `app_client_progress`, `app_trace`, `app_changed_actions`, `app_action_slips`, `app_status_pack`, `app_portfolio` and `app_recommendation_stances` are SECURITY INVOKER. The home stats use `actionConditions()`. The unread count runs under the `owner` SELECT policy. Today is firm-only. The digest skips hidden notices.
- Notices and mail: `hide_private_action` fires before `mail_action_notification` (BEFORE triggers run in name order), so a dropped notice sends no instant mail. Mention and comment mail go through `app_user_sees_action`.
- Files: the Files card and the evidence picker (`listFiles`, same code) keep a teammate's file under an unnamed "Evidence" folder. The app has no file page, global search or calendar feed.
