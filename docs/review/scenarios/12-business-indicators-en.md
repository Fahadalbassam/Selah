## English wording and layout
- All labels are English. The only Arabic is data (the indicator Arabic names, units typed in Arabic by the seed such as «شكوى» and «يوم», the recommendation titles).
- Confusing: **Left out** for the figure the import kept (it is the same row as "Kept (differs)"); **Correct figures that differ** as a switch with no hint of how many rows it would change (the count under **Corrections** stays 0 until it is on); "Figures: typed in" beside a card saying the way is not chosen.
- Good: the page-level sentence "A correction keeps the earlier value in the history, with its note."

## Four answers
1. **Could a real person do it alone?** Typing a figure: yes, in three clicks. The import: only after finding the hidden data-mode card; a person who goes looking for an import button first will not find one.
2. **Anything break, lose data, show the wrong thing or show something to the wrong person?** No. The typed figure survived the import; the observer and the client admin saw no editing or linking controls. Small: after **Confirm** (data mode) and after **Save 8 figures** the page does not refresh itself.
3. **Did Selah act like the consultant?** No. Every notice and page says "reported by the client, not verified impact"; no "worse" label; the link says it does not claim cause.
4. **One change:** show **Import figures** (greyed, with "choose how figures reach Selah first") before the data mode is chosen, and match a file label by name even when the unit in brackets differs.
