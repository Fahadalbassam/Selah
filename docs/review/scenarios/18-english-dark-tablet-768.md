## Layout at 768 px (what I looked for)
- No horizontal scroll on any visited page (`scrollWidth = innerWidth` = 768 on consent, invite, home, project, action, progress, help).
- The sidebar collapses to an icon rail with no labels; the first Tab stop (the logo link) and the icons have no visible text, which is why the focus names matter.
- The project title wraps over two lines with the client chip; the tab strip (Plan, Requests and files, Meetings, Progress, People) fits in one row.
- Table-like rows (actions) shorten long titles with "..." (for example "Board of trustees approves ..."), so the full title is not visible on the Plan tab at this width.

1. **Could a real person do it without help?** Dana and Yousef: yes. Majed would hesitate at the **Release to the owner now** switch (default on, near-invisible state) and at the owner picker that wants an Arabic name.

4. **One change:** make the check boxes and the switch show their state clearly in dark mode (a coloured fill when on, a visible tick) and say "On: Dana will be told now / Off: saved as a draft" in words next to the switch.
