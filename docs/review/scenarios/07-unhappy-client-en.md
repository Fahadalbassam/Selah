## English wording and layout
- All the screen text is English: **Help**, **Your consultants**, **Write to the firm's management**, **Send the message**, **Mark handled**, **Remove from the project**, **Staff on project**. Only the **عربي** switch and data are Arabic. Hint line under **Your consultants**: "For a question about one action, ask it in that action's discussion... For anything else, write to them at their work address" is the clearest sentence on the page.
- Confusing: "Works on it" (project role) next to "Observer: reads and comments, changes nothing" (the first reads like a statement, not a role). "Staff on project" is both the heading and the button.
- The sentence on Help "something about the work worries you and you'd rather not raise it with your consultant" is the right tone for an unhappy client.

## Four answers
1. **Could a real person do it without help?** Reem: yes, if she opens **Help**; nothing on her home points there. Sara: yes. Reema: yes (the extra sign-in after the invitation is the only surprise).
2. **Anything break, lose data, show the wrong thing or show something to the wrong person?** No. Omar lost access at once, his history stayed, Reem's message reached management only. Small: after **Mark handled** the card stayed until reload; one hydration warning on Reem's Help.
3. **Did Selah act like the consultant?** No. It told management, it did not judge the complaint or decide anything.
4. **One change:** tell the client's manager when her consultant changes (or put "Write to the firm's management" on the home), and name the firm and the inviter in the invitation mail.
