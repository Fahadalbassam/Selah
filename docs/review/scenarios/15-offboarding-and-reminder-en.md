## English wording and layout
- Labels are English throughout: **Archive client**, **Make export pack**, **Record handover**, **Confirm receipt**, **Restore client**, **Place legal hold**, **Your export pack**. Only the **عربي** switch and data are Arabic.
- Two near-twins: **Record handover** (firm, for a pack given outside Selah) and **Confirm receipt** (client). The first asks "Received by"; a firm admin could press it by mistake and mark the pack handed when the client has not got it.
- "Client can read until Nov 20, 2026 - Access ended" is clear; the empty **Grace period** label next to it is not.
