# Engagement modes and departments

How much a client takes part in Selah, and the optional departments that narrow who at the client sees what. Tables in `src/db/schema`, rules in the Engagement section of `drizzle/0000_baseline.sql`. Every rule below is enforced in Postgres; `src/services/engagement.ts` gives clear errors. These modes are the team's proposal from earlier discussions, not confirmed requirements of any one firm, and all three run in the same platform.

## The three modes

`organizations.engagement_mode` is set for every client (it is null for the firm) and starts as `full` unless chosen otherwise when the client is created (`POST /api/clients`) or when a won inquiry becomes a client (`POST /api/intake/inquiries/:id/convert`, `client.engagementMode`).

| | full | restricted | consultant_managed |
|---|---|---|---|
| Client accounts | any client role | client admins only, up to `firm_settings.restricted_max_admins` (2) | none |
| Who records progress and evidence | the client's people | the client admin | firm staff on the project, for the client |
| Client uploads | as chosen | private to the client until a client admin shares them | n/a |
| Reminders | the owner, then the ladder | the owner (an admin), then the ladder | the client's lead consultant at every level, then the watching firm admins |

- **Changing the mode.** `PATCH /api/orgs/:id/engagement` with `engagementMode`, by the firm admin only. Each change is in the audit log (`organization.engagement_mode`, with from and to). Nothing is deleted. A mode is refused while the client has accounts that do not fit it: switch the others off first (`PATCH /api/people/:id`), then change it.
- **Accounts.** Invitations and memberships are checked against the mode when they are made and again when an invitation is accepted, so a mode changed in between still holds.

## Consultant-managed: recording for the client

The client does not use Selah. Firm staff on its project record what they hear through normal consulting work.

- `POST /api/actions/:id/updates` and `POST /api/actions/:id/evidence` take `receivedVia` (`meeting`, `email`, `phone`, `document`) and `receivedOn` (a date, not in the future). Firm staff must give both; nobody else passes them.
- Every progress update and evidence submission has a `source` set by the database from the author's organisation: `client_portal`, `consultant_recorded`, or `internal` (an internal project). It is returned on the action's timeline, so screens and reports can show "recorded by a consultant" next to "submitted by the client" and never mix the two.
- Firm staff can record only for a consultant-managed client. In the other modes the client records its own work.
- An action's owner can be a name instead of an account: `ownerName` on `POST /api/recommendations/:id/actions` and `PATCH /api/actions/:id`, for consultant-managed clients only. Client contacts (`/api/clients/:id/contacts`) hold their details.
- **Separation of duties.** Whoever submitted evidence, or recorded progress for the client, since the last review cannot review that round. Someone else on the team verifies.

## Restricted: client-private files

- What a client admin uploads is stored `client_private`, whatever the upload asked for. Firm staff, the firm admin included, get a 404 for it, and the assistant never indexes it.
- `POST /api/documents/:id/share` (client admin only) shares it with the firm. Sharing is one-way: a file the firm has seen is never made private again. The share is recorded in the action history (`document_shared`) and the audit log.
- Evidence is shared by submitting it, because the firm must see it to verify. Evidence cannot point at a client-private file.
- Be honest in the UI: these files are "private from the firm's users in Selah". The firm still runs the server.
- Switching a restricted client to full keeps its private files private.

## Departments

Optional and off by default (`organizations.departments_enabled`). Nobody is turned away for not using them.

- `PATCH /api/orgs/:id/engagement` with `departmentsEnabled` switches them on or off (the client admin or the firm admin; audited as `organization.departments`).
- `GET /api/orgs/:id/engagement` returns the mode, the flag and the departments with their people. `POST /api/orgs/:id/departments`, `PATCH /api/departments/:id` (rename, or `active: false`), `PUT /api/departments/:id/members/:userId` (with `isHead`) and `DELETE` manage them. Members are active people of that client.
- An action or document can carry `departmentId`. When departments are on, it is visible only to the department's members, the client's admins, the firm staff who can see the project, and the action's owner (or the file's uploader). Everyone else gets a 404. An action owner, whatever the department, reads only the actions she owns (decision 1b; docs/authorization.md, "Action owners see only their own actions"); her department's files stay visible to her. Progress, evidence, evidence requests and reviews follow their action, and an evidence file takes its action's department.
- A recommendation and a meeting can carry one too. A department's recommendation is read by its department, the client's admins, its accountable person and the owners of its actions; a department's meeting, with everything under it, by its department, the client's admins, its organiser and anyone invited; a meeting of the whole project only by people who may see all of the project's work, and action owners invited to it (decision 1a, docs/meetings.md).
- The promise holds on every screen (decision D8): nobody is shown, by title, work they may not open. Lists that leave something out say how many, never what; the full list of places is in docs/authorization.md, "What a person with limits never sees by title". The Departments page lists every department with its people for everyone at the client, and links a card to the department's page only for those who may open it.
- Switched off, everyone on the project sees everything again, except that an action owner still reads only her own actions. The department column stays.
- Level 2 of the reminder ladder adds the head of the action's department. A head who is an action owner cannot open a colleague's action (1b), so when no head of the department can open it, the client's admins are told at that step in the head's place, and the action's ladder says so ("Client admins, in place of the department head, who can't open this action"; migration `0088_head_escalation`). A head who is a manager, or who owns the action, is told as before.
- The assistant's index copies each chunk's department, and retrieval applies the same rule inside the vector search. Refiling a document or an action takes its chunks out until it is indexed again.
- A department cuts across projects (HR work shows up in several), so it does not repeat the project scope.

## Client groups

A holding group (F34, decision D4) is a record of its own (`client_groups`: Arabic and English names, firm-only notes), kept by the firm admin. The client stays the tenant boundary, because a holding and a subsidiary are separate controllers under PDPL; a group only pools counts.

- Each member company takes one of two forms, chosen per company. **A linked client** (`client_group_members`, one group per client at most) keeps its data, people and files apart, as for a listed subsidiary. **A company inside a client** is one of that client's departments marked `is_company` by the firm admin while the client is in a group: it shares the client's people and files, and its numbers roll up into the client's. Unlinking a client turns its companies back into departments.
- `POST /api/client-groups`, `PATCH` and `DELETE /api/client-groups/:id`, `POST /api/client-groups/:id/members` (`clientOrgId`), `DELETE /api/client-groups/:id/members/:clientId`, and `PUT` or `DELETE /api/client-groups/:id/companies/:departmentId`. All are the firm admin's and audited (`client_group.*`).
- The group page pools the counts of its member companies from `app_client_progress()` under the reader's row-level security, with a row per company: each company inside a client from its department's row, and the client's own row from the rest. The percentage is worked out again from the pooled counts. No group KPIs: those wait on F35.
- The firm admin sees every group. A consultant sees a group only while assigned to one of its clients, and only those clients and their numbers. The client side reads none of these tables.
- **The group on the client side.** The firm admin names one member the holding (`client_group_members.is_holding`, `PUT /api/client-groups/:id/holding`) and grants named admins or managers of the holding the group's roll-up (`client_group_viewers`, `POST /api/client-groups/:id/viewers`, `DELETE .../viewers/:userId`; audited). They see it as a section of their home (page audit section 7, item 8; the old `/group` sends them there): the pooled counts with a row per member company, from `app_client_group_rollup()`, which counts only what the firm and each company both see (released actions, projects past draft, shared recommendations). They open nothing of a linked company: its records stay behind its own row-level security. Nobody else at a member company sees the group or a sibling. A new holding ends the old holding's grants.

## Minimal records (consultant-managed only)

Some family offices want the firm to keep as little as possible. `PATCH /api/orgs/:id/engagement` with `minimalRecords: true` and a `displayAlias` (firm admin only; baseline: Insight):

- The alias replaces the client's name in Selah. Switching it on also clears the inquiry the client came from: the organisation's name and CR number, the requester's contact details and message, the discovery session's attendees and notes, and the notes in the inquiry's history. The inquiry keeps its link to the client and its statistics (sector, services, size, ownership, dates). Audit entries hold ids, not names.
- It is switched on before anything is filed: the client must have no contacts or files yet, and its organisation structure must hold nobody's name, now or in the past (`position_holders`, migration 0091). Nothing is deleted to make way for it: the switch is refused until they are gone (`check_minimal_records()`).
- From then on Selah refuses, for that client: files (so evidence is recorded as progress with `receivedVia`), client contacts, assistant indexing, and an account as an action's owner. `ownerName` holds a job title ("finance manager"), not a person's name.
- Leaving consultant-managed mode is refused while it is on.
- `npm run job:purge-minimal` (daily, as the schema owner) blanks free text once `firm_settings.minimal_purge_days` (90) have passed since `projects.handover_on`. Each project is purged once.
  - Blanked: progress updates, firm notes on actions, meeting agendas, notes, decisions and summaries, finding descriptions, recommendation rationale, target outcome and required evidence, action descriptions, closure notes and blocked reasons, and assessment answers' written text and comments.
  - Kept: titles (of the project, findings, recommendations, actions and meetings), owner job titles, statuses, dates, and who did what. Consultants write titles without names or sensitive detail.
- **Not for a client who forbids any record.** Minimal records keeps less, for less long, but Selah still stores the work while it runs. A client who does not allow its information to be stored at all should not be managed in Selah. An alias does not change that: the firm still holds the record.

## Deliverables handed over, and the client's receipt

F21 in its minimal form (gate A follow-ups, 17 Nov 2026, 4a; migration `0092_deliverable_receipts`, service `src/services/deliverables.ts`). A signed engagement lists its deliverables (`engagement_deliverables`: title, how the client accepts it, due date, the project that delivers it). Each is now handed over and received in rounds (`deliverable_handovers`): one row per round with the exact file versions (`version_ids`, documents ids), who handed it over and when, and the client's answer.

- **Hand over** (`POST /api/deliverables/:id/hand-over` with `versionIds`; `GET` lists the files). The client's lead, deputy or a consultant staffed (not as an observer) on a project of the deliverable's engagement that is not closed (staffing under another engagement of the client does not count) picks deliverable files (`documents.purpose = 'deliverable'`) of the engagement's projects, each at its latest version unless an earlier one is chosen. Before saving, the dialog says who at the client will be asked (`POST /api/deliverables/:id/hand-over/preview`), and names an acceptor skipped because they cannot open every file picked. The files must already be shared with the client, live and cleared by the scan. A hand-over never shares anything: a file still firm-only is not offered, and the dialog names it in one line and points to its own Share action, which asks and warns first. The service and the database refuse a firm-only version. A new version means a new round; every round is kept.
- **Who is asked.** The acceptors (client role `acceptor`) on the projects the deliverable relates to, else the client's admins, else its sponsor; only people who open every file. They get one notice (`deliverable.handed_over`) and a work mail, both opening `/deliverables`.
- **The answer** (`POST /api/deliverables/:id/receipt` or `/comment`, with the `round` they saw): an acceptor, admin or sponsor confirms receipt (who and when are kept against the round's versions, so a later version changes nothing), or writes a comment (required). A comment reaches the client's lead and deputy (`deliverable.commented`, and the decisions list on the lead's home until a new version is handed over). One answer per round, on the latest round only.
- **A client the firm runs** (consultant_managed): nobody is asked. Its lead, deputy or a staffed consultant records the receipt with who at the client received it, how (`meeting`, `email`, `phone`, `document`) and the day, between the hand-over and today; the receipt says it was recorded by the firm (`receipt_source = 'consultant_recorded'`), like recorded evidence.
- **One word per deliverable**, from its latest round, the same for every reader (also one who cannot open that round's files; they see its state, never its files, and their own older round marked replaced): Not handed over (لم يُسلَّم), Handed over (waiting) (سُلِّم (بانتظار التأكيد)), Received (استُلم), Commented (عليه ملاحظة); after its project closed, a round never answered reads No answer before the project closed (لم يصل رد قبل إغلاق المشروع). No percentage. Received is receipt only, never acceptance; the screens, mails and PDFs say so.
- **One reminder.** A round still waiting after 10 Saudi working days reminds its receivers once (`deliverable.reminder`), through the summary mail and its mail rules (`app_remind_deliverables()`, run by the daily follow-up job). Working days are Sunday to Thursday (Friday and Saturday off, as the mail rules hold them); public holidays (the two Eids, Founding Day, National Day) are counted as working days, since Selah has no holiday calendar, so a reminder can come a little early or on a holiday. The job counts from the hand-over's day and sends on the morning of the tenth working day after it.
- **Closing.** Nothing is written on a closed project's deliverables. The Closing tab warns, without blocking, about rounds still waiting; after closing, such a round reads "No answer before the project closed" (لم يصل رد قبل إغلاق المشروع) everywhere it is shown, and is never reminded.
- **Records.** The closure report lists the project's deliverables with their receipt; the export pack has `deliverables.csv` with every round. A deliverable that was handed over can no longer be removed, and a file handed over can no longer be withdrawn. An archived client's rounds freeze.
- Not built (decision 4a): comments pinned to pages of a draft, side-by-side versions, e-signature, approval workflows on drafts, any link to invoicing.

## Screens

- **The client page** (`/clients/:id`) has a "How the client takes part" card for firm staff. The firm admin changes the mode there. Before saving, the dialog says what stands in the way of each mode: the number of active accounts, the accounts that are not client admins, the restricted limit, or minimal records being on (`getEngagement` returns this as `fit`, for the firm admin only). The database still decides, and its refusals are shown in the person's language (`engagementError` in `src/lib/engagement.ts`). Minimal records are switched on there too, with an alias, and only while the client has no contacts or files and no names in its organisation structure; the card says which stands in the way ("The client's structure still holds names of people. Remove them from the structure first.").
- **Departments**: `/clients/:id/departments` for the firm, and `/departments` for the client's admins and managers, a tab of the company's People page (`/team`, with the Structure; page audit 4.1). The client admin always has the tab (the switch is on it); a manager has it while departments are on. Admins (the firm admin or the client admin) switch departments on or off, add, rename and switch off departments, and add or remove people and heads. Managers see the list. Each department card shows one button, "Add a person"; renaming, moving its work and switching it off are in the card's menu (⋯), and making someone head or taking them out in each person's menu (batch 1, page audit U15).
- **Client groups**: the second view of the Clients page (`/clients?view=groups`; the old `/clients/groups` redirects there) and `/clients/groups/:id` for the firm. The admin creates and edits groups, links and unlinks clients, marks companies inside a client, names the holding and gives its people access; consultants read the groups of their clients. A section of the home for the holding's people who were given access (`/group` redirects there; their sidebar's Our group row opens it).
- **Deliverables** (F21): the engagement page (`/engagements/:id`, the firm and the client's admin) shows each deliverable's status word, its latest hand-over with the files, **Hand over / Hand over a new version** for the firm, **Record receipt** for a client the firm runs, **Confirm receipt** and **Comment** for the client's receiver, and the earlier rounds folded underneath. The client page's engagements card counts each engagement's deliverables by status word. A client person who answers for a hand-over sees, on the home page (`/`), only the rounds waiting for their answer (card "Files waiting for your confirmation of receipt", one main action: Confirm receipt), with a link to all of them on `/deliverables`; nothing when none wait.
- **The action page** shows the action's department while the client uses departments. Firm staff who can change the plan file it under a department there, or under none.
- **A client the firm runs** (consultant_managed): its project and action pages carry a muted line, "The firm records this work on the client's behalf". The action page's progress-note form and its file dialogs (attach evidence, send for review, an evidence-list item's "Send a file") ask how the client sent it and when (a day, the firm's today by default, never later) and send both; a refusal from the server shows in the dialog. Each progress note and piece of evidence the firm recorded says in the action's history how and when the client sent it ("Received from the client by email on 12 Nov"). The action's texts never ask the client to sign in, send or read anything: the evidence list, its dialogs and the discussion use their firm-run wording, and the reminder ladder names only the lead consultant and the watching firm admins.
- Files are not filed under a department from a screen yet. Evidence takes its action's department.
