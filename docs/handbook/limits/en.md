# What Selah will not do

One page for anyone who shows Selah to a client, so nobody promises what it does not do. Selah connects, records and shows; people always decide.

[العربية](ar.md) · [Handbook front page](../en.md)

## 1. No consulting advice

- It does not write recommendations, suggest a strategy or analyse strengths and weaknesses. The firm's consultants write the recommendations.
- It does not judge the quality of the client's work or its organisation structure. The structure in Selah describes positions only, and never grants or limits access.
- What the assistant writes is a draft or an answer from sources, not the firm's view until a consultant takes it on.

## 2. No compliance ruling

- It never says a client is "compliant" or "non-compliant" with any law or standard. An assessment shows the gaps the consultant saw, and its scores stay with the firm.
- The assistant refuses legal and compliance rulings, for the firm and for the client.
- The terms, privacy notice and data processing agreement in Selah are drafts for the firm's counsel to review before anyone relies on them.

## 3. No automatic approvals

- No action becomes Verified without a consultant's review. The database itself refuses it.
- Nobody reviews work they own or sent evidence for. A second sign-off comes from another consultant.
- What the client sends is a claim that the work is done until it is reviewed. "Waiting for the firm" does not mean "done".
- "Received" on a deliverable means the files arrived, never that the client accepted the work.
- The client's answer to a recommendation is the client's record, not the firm's view.
- The assistant accepts no evidence, changes no status and sends no message.

## 4. The assistant's limits

- It answers only from what the person asking may open, and names the source of every answer. It can be wrong: open the source before relying on an answer.
- It reads a file only once its owner allows it and the file is scanned. It does not read scanned pages (there is no text recognition for images yet).
- When the model is hosted outside the server, the names Selah knows (people, clients, departments, cities, emails, phone numbers) are replaced with placeholders before the text leaves. Names Selah does not know, inside a file's text, are not replaced, and the hosted model reads the passages Selah sends it.
- The client assistant stays off until the firm offers it and the client admin agrees, has a daily question limit, and is never available to action owners. It never sees the firm's notes, assessment scores or other clients.
- It does not guess the firm's intentions or speak for it.

## 5. Numbers are not impact

- Business indicators are figures the client reports, not verified impact. Every screen says so: "These figures are reported by the client and are not verified impact. A change in an indicator is not proof that a recommendation caused it." The client reads: "Your company's reported figures, not verified impact."
- Linking an indicator to a recommendation means the consultant expects it to move the number, not that it caused a change.
- An "indicator moved away" notice gives the figures only: no "worse", no colour, no forecast, no thresholds.
- The share the consultant verified measures implementation that was reviewed, not business results.
- Selah never describes a project's health in a word ("off track", "on track"): counts only. It never ranks or scores consultants or people.

## 6. It is not

- A general file drive, a dashboard of charts, a chatbot or an automated consultant.
- A call host or a calendar sync: it sends the calendar invitation and the call link only.
- An invoicing, HR or finance system.

## What to say instead

| Don't say | Say |
|---|---|
| "Selah checks that the work is done" | "Our consultants review the evidence in Selah, and Selah records their decision" |
| "Selah measures the impact of our recommendations" | "Selah shows your figures, as you report them, beside the work; they are not verified impact" |
| "The assistant answers any question" | "The assistant answers from what you may open, names its source, and gives no rulings" |
| "Selah tells you whether you are compliant" | "The assessment shows the gaps the consultant saw" |
| "Confirming receipt means you accepted the deliverable" | "Confirming receipt only means the files arrived" |
